Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026. Short on purpose: this is exactly what is stored and where.

What is stored, and where

DataWhereWhy
Email address and password hashSupabase Auth (hosted database, EU/US region chosen at project creation)To sign you in. We never see your password; Supabase stores only a hash.
Display nameSupabase (profiles table)Shown in the app and on a Plan you choose to share.
Tracks, Plans, items, schedules, completionsSupabase, rows tagged with your user id and protected by row-level securityThis is the app. Only your account can read or change your rows.
Review notes and today’s focusYour device first, then Supabase (synced-state table), rows tagged with your user id and protected by row-level securitySo your notes and focus are the same on your phone and on the web. Only your account can read or change them.
Reminders, notification settings, preferences, skips, onboarding progressYour device only (browser storage or the phone app’s local database)They never leave the device. Signing out removes reminders; clearing site data removes the rest.
Usage events and error reportsSupabase (client-events table), tagged with your user idTo find bugs and see which features are used: for example “tour finished”, “Plan created”, or the technical message and stack trace of a crash, with the app version. Never your item names, notes or anything else you type. On by default; switch it off in Settings → Preferences → “Share usage and error reports”.
Share linksSupabase (plan_shares table)Only when you create one. Anyone with the link sees that one Plan, read-only, until you revoke it.

What is not collected

  • No third-party analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising identifiers, no third-party cookies. The usage events above are first-party: they go to the same database as your account and nowhere else.
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone access.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone. There is no AI model trained on your data.

Sign-in with Google or Apple

If you use it, the provider tells Supabase your email and name so an account can be created or matched. Nothing is posted to your Google or Apple account.

Your controls

  • Export everything as JSON or CSV from Settings → Your data.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Delete account. It removes your sign-in and every row you own immediately and permanently, including synced notes and usage events.
  • Stop usage and error reports at any time in Settings → Preferences.
  • Revoke any share link from the Plan it belongs to.
